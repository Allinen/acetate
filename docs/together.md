# Listening together

**Together** (in the sidebar, or `go:together` in the palette) is for listening in step with
other people on the same server, and for seeing what they listen to.

## A shared session

One person **starts a session** and sends the **invite code** it shows (`Copy`) to whoever
should join; they type it under **Join**. The code is the only way in: nobody can find or
join a session without it. Up to 20 people fit in one.

In a session everyone hears the same track at the same place, and anyone can:

- play something else (an album, a playlist, a search), skip, or go back;
- pause, resume or seek;
- add to or change the queue.

Whatever one person does, the others' players do too. If two people act at the same moment
the later one gives way to the earlier. A track that ends moves everyone on together.

Each person plays from their own copy of the library, so a track someone can't see (it's
restricted to groups they're not in) doesn't play for them: they stay quiet on it and follow
along again at the next track. Only library tracks are shared; a file that didn't import, or
an extra, plays for you alone.

**Leave** takes you out of the session and your music goes on by itself. When the last person
leaves, or everyone has been gone for a minute or so, the session ends. Sessions are kept in
the server's memory, so restarting the server ends them. A shared list holds at most 10 000
tracks; a longer one is played by you alone.

Joining needs the permission to stream.

## What others are listening to

Under **Listening now** are the tracks other people on the server are playing. Click one to
open its page, or **Play** to hear it yourself. Only tracks you may see are shown.

This is **off by default**: nothing about what you play is told to the server until you tick
**Let others see what I'm listening to**. It's remembered on this computer. Switching it off
takes you out of the list at once. Being in a session doesn't change it.
